## 🎓 Programs & Curriculum

### 1. Deaf Learners Program
**Structure:**
- **Preprimary**: Ages 3–7
- **Foundation Phase**: Grade R–3
- **Intermediate Phase**: Grade 4–6
- **Senior Phase**: Grade 7–9
- **FET Phase**: Grade 10–12
- **Technical Skills Development**

**Curriculum:**
- SASL (South African Sign Language)
- All CAPS subjects adapted for deaf learners
- Technical skills: Welding, Woodworking, Hospitality

### 2. Blind Learners Program
**Structure:**
- **Preprimary**: Ages 4–7
- **Foundation to Senior Phase**: Grade R–8
- **FET**: Grade 10–12
- **Technical Skills Development**

**Curriculum:**
- Braille literacy
- Mobility and orientation training
- Adaptive technology
- Life skills and independence training

### 3. Autistic Learners Program
**Structure:**
- **Level 1–4**: Individualized progression
- **Skills 1, 2, 3**: Practical life skills

**Curriculum:**
- Sensory integration
- Social skills development
- Communication therapy
- Structured learning environment

### 4. Intellectual Barriers Program
**Structure:**
- **Level 1–6**: Ability-based progression

**Curriculum:**
- Life skills training
- Creative arts and crafts
- Basic hospitality skills
- Gardening and agriculture
- Mathematics and literacy at appropriate levels

### Technical Skills Development (PVT Section)

**Academic Branch:**
- Mathematics, English, SASL, Sesotho
- ICT and Life Skills

**Practical Subjects:**
- **Mechanical Skills**: Welding, Metalwork
- **Construction**: Bricklaying, Woodworking
- **Hospitality**: Food Production, Cookery
- **Creative Arts**: Needlework, Pottery, Cane-work
- **Agriculture**: Vegetable Garden
- **Animal Husbandry**: Cattle & Piggery

## 🏫 Admissions

### Admission Criteria
- Age-appropriate assessment
- Medical and psychological evaluations
- Individual education plan development
- Family consultation and support

### Application Process
1. **Initial Inquiry**: Contact school directly  
2. **Assessment**: Multi-disciplinary team evaluation  
3. **Documentation**: Medical reports, previous school records  
4. **Placement**: Program assignment based on individual needs  
5. **Orientation**: Family and learner orientation program  

### Required Documents
- Birth certificate
- Medical reports
- Previous school records
- Parent/guardian identification
- Proof of residence

### Fees and Financial Support
- Government subsidized education
- Additional support available for disadvantaged families
- Hostel accommodation available
