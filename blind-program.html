Blind Learners Program

Comprehensive education for visually impaired learners using Braille literacy, mobility training, and adaptive technology to achieve academic excellence and independence.

View Curriculum Apply Now

Program Overview

Empowering independence through excellence in blind education

Why Choose Our Blind Program?

  • Braille Excellence: Comprehensive Braille literacy from foundation level
  • Mobility Training: Orientation and mobility skills for independence
  • Adaptive Technology: Screen readers and assistive technology integration
  • Academic Success: Full CAPS curriculum adapted for visual learners
  • Technical Skills: Practical training for career readiness
  • Life Skills Focus: Daily living skills and independence training

Technical Skills Development

Practical training adapted for blind learners, focusing on independence and employability

Adaptive Technology

Comprehensive training in assistive technology including screen readers, Braille displays, and computer skills for modern workplace integration.

  • JAWS screen reader proficiency
  • Braille display operation
  • Computer navigation skills
  • Internet and email usage
Hospitality & Food Service

Adapted culinary arts and hospitality training emphasizing safety, organization, and professional food service skills.

  • Safe cooking techniques
  • Kitchen organization systems
  • Food safety standards
  • Customer service skills
Traditional Crafts

Traditional craft skills including cane work, needlework, and pottery, developing fine motor skills and entrepreneurship opportunities.

  • Cane work and basketry
  • Advanced needlework
  • Pottery and ceramics
  • Product marketing
Music & Audio Arts

Leveraging natural audio strengths through music education, audio production, and performance skills.

  • Musical instrument training
  • Audio recording basics
  • Performance skills
Agriculture & Horticulture

Adapted agricultural training focusing on tactile identification, sustainable farming, and garden management.

  • Plant identification by touch
  • Soil preparation techniques
  • Organic farming methods

Specialized Support Services

Comprehensive support ensuring success for every blind learner

Braille & Print Services

Dedicated Braille committee ensuring quality Braille production, tactile graphics, and specialized learning materials.

Orientation & Mobility

Professional mobility training including white cane techniques, route planning, and safe independent travel skills.

Assistive Technology

State-of-the-art adaptive technology including screen readers, Braille displays, and specialized software training.

Daily Living Skills

Comprehensive training in personal care, household management, and independent living skills for full autonomy.

Low Vision Support

Specialized services for learners with partial sight, including magnification tools and visual enhancement techniques.
