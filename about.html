About Thiboloha Special School

50 years of transforming lives through excellence in special needs education

Our Story

Building bridges where others see barriers

Established in 1974, Thiboloha Special School has been a beacon of hope and excellence in special needs education for half a century. Located in the scenic Witsieshoek area of the Free State, nestled in the Drakensberg mountains, our school serves as a comprehensive Resource Centre for Learners with Barriers to Learning and Development.

What makes Thiboloha unique is our holistic approach to special needs education. We are one of the few institutions in South Africa that provides specialized programs for deaf, blind, autistic, and intellectually impaired learners all under one roof, creating a truly inclusive environment where every child can thrive.

Our consistent 100% National Senior Certificate pass rate from 2011 to 2024 stands as testament to our unwavering commitment to academic excellence and our belief that every learner, regardless of their challenges, deserves the opportunity to succeed.

Our Foundation

The principles that guide everything we do

Our Mission

To provide excellence in special needs education, empowering learners with disabilities to reach their full potential through innovative teaching methods, comprehensive support services, and a nurturing environment that celebrates diversity and promotes inclusion.

Our Vision

To be the leading center of excellence in special needs education in Southern Africa, where every learner with disabilities is equipped with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to become productive, independent, and valued members of society.

Our Values

  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards
  • Inclusion: Celebrating diversity and differences
  • Empowerment: Building confidence and independence
  • Compassion: Caring for each individual
  • Innovation: Embracing new approaches

Our Journey

Five decades of growth, innovation, and impact

1974
Foundation

Thiboloha Special School was established in Witsieshoek, Free State, as a specialized institution for learners with disabilities, beginning our mission to provide quality education for those who needed it most.

1980s
Program Expansion

Expanded services to include comprehensive programs for deaf, blind, and intellectually impaired learners, establishing ourselves as a multi-disability educational center.

1990s
Technical Skills Integration

Introduced technical skills development programs including woodworking, metalwork, hospitality, and agriculture, preparing students for employment opportunities.

2000s
Academic Excellence

Achieved recognition for outstanding academic results and began our journey toward consistent 100% NSC pass rates, proving that barriers to learning can be overcome.

2011-2024
Sustained Excellence

Maintained 100% NSC pass rates for 14 consecutive years, with many graduates achieving Bachelor and Diploma passes, demonstrating our commitment to academic excellence.

2024
Golden Jubilee Approach

As we approach our 50th anniversary, we continue to innovate and expand, with plans for new facilities and enhanced support services for our learners.

Comprehensive Support Services

A network of care ensuring holistic development for every learner

Professional Support Staff

Our dedicated team includes a Social Worker for counseling and family support, Audiologists for hearing assessments, and a School Nurse for health and medical care. We are actively recruiting an Occupational Therapist to complete our multidisciplinary team.

School-Based Support Team (SBST)

Our SBST provides coordinated support for learners with additional needs, implementing individualized education plans and ensuring seamless collaboration between teachers, specialists, and families.

SASL & Braille Committee

Specialized committees ensure excellence in South African Sign Language instruction and Braille literacy, maintaining high standards in communication accessibility for our deaf and blind learners.

Safety & Security

Our Safety Committee ensures a secure learning environment with comprehensive safety protocols, emergency procedures, and ongoing risk assessments to protect all learners and staff.

Hostel Accommodation

Quality residential facilities provide a home away from home for learners who travel from distant areas, with dedicated hostel staff ensuring proper care, supervision, and support.

Learner Leadership Development

Through our Learners' Committee, Representative Council of Learners (RCL), and Group Leaders, we develop leadership skills and give students a voice in their educational experience.

Educational Partnerships

Collaborative support from education district and community partners

District Support Services

  • Subject Advisors visit regularly for curriculum support and teacher development
  • Human Resources support with educator appointments and substitute teachers
  • Professional Development through ongoing teacher training programs
  • Educational Camps subsidized winter and September learning camps
  • SIAS Implementation workshops for School-Based Support Teams
  • Learner Support Facilitators for specialized educational interventions

Community Impact

Transforming lives and building inclusive communities

Rural Education Excellence

Located in Witsieshoek, Free State, we serve rural communities often underserved by specialized educational facilities, bringing world-class special needs education to the Drakensberg region.

Alumni Success Network

Our graduates work across South Africa in universities, hospitals, courts, and businesses, serving as ambassadors for disability inclusion and proving that barriers can become bridges to success.

Community Recognition

Supported by organizations like the Hollywood Foundation and recognized for our educational excellence, we continue to attract partnerships that enhance opportunities for our learners.

Looking Ahead

Our vision for continued growth and excellence

As we approach our golden jubilee, Thiboloha Special School continues to evolve and expand. Our strategic development plans focus on enhancing facilities and services to better serve our learners and the broader community.

Infrastructure Development Goals:
  • Four additional mobile classrooms
  • Dedicated Autistic learners center
  • Modern science laboratory
  • Enhanced hospitality training center
  • Expanded skills workshop building
  • Specialized infrastructure for blind learners

These developments will enable us to serve more learners, offer enhanced programs, and maintain our position as a leader in special needs education while preserving the caring, family-like atmosphere that defines Thiboloha.
