Deaf Learners Program

Comprehensive education using South African Sign Language (SASL) from preprimary through FET, empowering deaf learners to achieve academic excellence and career success.

View Curriculum Apply Now

Program Overview

Excellence in deaf education through innovative teaching and SASL immersion

Why Choose Our Deaf Program?

  • 100% NSC Pass Rate: Consistent academic excellence from 2011-2024
  • SASL Immersion: Full curriculum delivery in South African Sign Language
  • Technical Skills: Practical training in welding, woodworking, and hospitality
  • University Preparation: Bachelor pass achievements opening tertiary opportunities
  • Deaf Culture Integration: Celebrating and preserving deaf identity and community
  • Career Pathways: Alumni working in universities, businesses, and skilled trades

Technical Skills Development

Practical training preparing students for immediate employment and entrepreneurship

Welding & Metalwork

Professional welding techniques and metalworking skills preparing students for careers in construction, manufacturing, and repair industries.

  • Arc welding techniques
  • Metal fabrication
  • Safety protocols
  • Equipment maintenance
Woodworking & Construction

Comprehensive woodworking and construction skills including furniture making, carpentry, and building techniques.

  • Furniture construction
  • Carpentry techniques
  • Tool usage and maintenance
  • Project planning
Hospitality & Food Production

Professional cooking, baking, and hospitality management skills preparing students for careers in the food service industry.

  • Professional cooking
  • Baking and pastry
  • Food safety standards
  • Customer service
Agriculture & Horticulture

Sustainable farming practices, vegetable gardening, and agricultural business principles.

  • Vegetable production
  • Soil management
  • Sustainable practices
Creative Arts & Crafts

Traditional and contemporary crafts including needlework, pottery, and cane work for cultural preservation and entrepreneurship.

  • Traditional needlework
  • Pottery and ceramics
  • Cane work and basketry
