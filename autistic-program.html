Specialized Teaching Approaches

Evidence-based strategies tailored for learners on the autism spectrum

Visual Learning Supports

Comprehensive visual support systems including picture schedules, social stories, and visual cues to enhance understanding and communication.

  • Picture Exchange Communication
  • Visual schedules and timers
  • Social stories and scripts
  • Environmental visual cues
Sensory Integration Therapy

Specialized sensory programs addressing sensory processing challenges and creating optimal learning environments for each individual.

  • Sensory assessment and profiling
  • Sensory diet implementation
  • Calm-down spaces and tools
  • Movement and proprioceptive activities
Positive Behavior Support

Proactive behavior intervention strategies focusing on understanding and meeting the underlying needs behind challenging behaviors.

  • Functional behavior assessment
  • Replacement behavior teaching
  • Environmental modifications
  • Positive reinforcement systems
Communication Development

Comprehensive communication support including speech therapy, AAC systems, and social communication skill development.

  • Augmentative communication devices
  • Speech and language therapy
  • Social communication groups
Social Skills Training

Structured social skills instruction through role-playing, peer interaction opportunities, and real-world practice sessions.

  • Peer interaction activities
  • Social skills groups
  • Community-based learning

Comprehensive Support Services

Holistic support ensuring success for autistic learners and their families

Individual Education Plans

Comprehensive IEPs developed collaboratively with families, outlining specific goals, strategies, and progress monitoring for each learner.

Family Training & Support

Regular training sessions for families on autism strategies, behavior management, and home-school collaboration techniques.

Multidisciplinary Team

Collaborative team including special education teachers, speech therapists, occupational therapists, and behavioral specialists.

Progress Monitoring

Regular assessment and data collection to track progress, adjust interventions, and celebrate achievements in all developmental areas.

Transition Planning

Comprehensive planning for transitions between levels and preparation for post-school life, including vocational and independent living skills.
