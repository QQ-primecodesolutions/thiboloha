Intellectual Barriers Program

Life skills-focused education promoting independence, community integration, and meaningful participation for learners with intellectual disabilities through individualized, practical learning approaches.

View Program Levels Apply Now

Program Overview

Empowering independence through practical, meaningful life skills education

Why Choose Our Intellectual Barriers Program?

  • Individualized Pacing: Learning progresses at each student's optimal pace
  • Practical Life Skills: Focus on skills needed for daily independence
  • Community Integration: Real-world learning and social participation
  • Vocational Preparation: Work skills and entrepreneurship training
  • Creative Expression: Arts, crafts, and cultural activities
  • Family Partnership: Comprehensive family support and training

Program Levels

Ability-based progression supporting individual growth and development

Level 1
Foundation Skills
Basic Independence
Essential Life Skills Foundation

Introduction to basic personal care, communication, and social skills with emphasis on routine establishment and safety awareness.

Core Learning Areas:
Personal Care
Hygiene, dressing, and basic self-care routines
Basic Communication
Expressing needs and understanding simple instructions
Safety Awareness
Understanding safety rules and hazard recognition
Level 2
Functional Academics
Basic Literacy & Numeracy
Functional Academic Skills

Development of basic literacy and numeracy skills integrated with practical applications in daily life contexts.

Academic Areas:
Functional Literacy
Reading signs, labels, and basic written instructions
Practical Mathematics
Money concepts, time, and basic measurement
Languages
Sesotho and English for everyday communication
Level 3
Domestic Skills
Household Management
Household & Domestic Skills

Comprehensive training in household management, cooking, cleaning, and basic home maintenance skills.

Domestic Skills:
Cooking & Food Preparation
Basic cooking skills and food safety
Cleaning & Maintenance
Household cleaning and basic maintenance tasks
Laundry & Clothing Care
Washing, drying, and clothing maintenance
Level 4
Community Skills
Social Integration
Community Integration Skills

Development of skills needed for successful participation in community activities and social interactions.

Community Skills:
Shopping & Banking
Independent shopping and basic banking skills
Transportation
Using public transport and navigation skills
Social Interaction
Community participation and social relationships
Level 5
Vocational Skills
Work Preparation
Work Skills & Entrepreneurship

Preparation for employment through practical work skills, job training, and entrepreneurship development.

Vocational Areas:
Gardening & Agriculture
Vegetable production and agricultural skills
Hospitality Services
Food service and hospitality industry skills
Creative Arts & Crafts
Pottery, needlework, and craft production

Advanced Independence
Life Mastery
Advanced Life Management

Comprehensive independence skills including decision-making, problem-solving, and advanced community participation.

Advanced Skills:
Decision Making
Problem-solving and independent choices
Advocacy & Self-Representation
Self-advocacy and rights awareness
Independent Living
Full household and life management skills

Practical Skills Training

Hands-on learning opportunities developing employable skills and entrepreneurship

Creative Arts & Crafts

Traditional and contemporary arts and crafts training developing fine motor skills, creativity, and marketable products.

  • Pottery and ceramics
  • Traditional needlework
  • Painting and drawing
  • Product design and marketing
Gardening & Agriculture

Comprehensive agricultural training including vegetable production, sustainable farming practices, and agricultural business principles.

  • Vegetable garden management
  • Soil preparation and planting
  • Crop harvesting and storage
  • Agricultural tool usage
Hospitality & Food Service

Practical hospitality training including food preparation, service skills, and basic catering for employment opportunities.

  • Basic cooking and baking
  • Food safety and hygiene
  • Table service and presentation
  • Kitchen organization
Entrepreneurship Training

Business skills development including product creation, pricing, sales techniques, and small business management.

  • Product development and pricing
  • Basic sales and customer service
  • Money handling and record keeping
Community Service Skills

Training in community service roles including assistance work, cleaning services, and supportive employment opportunities.

  • Workplace behavior and etiquette
  • Following instructions and routines
  • Teamwork and cooperation

Comprehensive Support Services

Holistic support ensuring success for learners with intellectual barriers

Individual Assessment

Comprehensive assessment determining each learner's abilities, interests, and appropriate level placement for optimal learning outcomes.

Family Support & Training

Regular family training sessions on supporting independence at home, behavior management, and continuation of school learning.

Progress Monitoring

Continuous assessment and tracking of skill development with regular progress reports and goal adjustment as needed.

Social Skills Development

Structured social skills training through group activities, peer interaction, and community-based learning opportunities.

Transition Planning

Comprehensive preparation for post-school life including employment placement, independent living support, and ongoing community connections.
