Our Four Specialized Programs

Each program is designed with specific methodologies and support systems

Deaf Learners Program

SASL Education & Academic Excellence

Education Span: Preprimary - FET (13 years)
Primary Language: South African Sign Language
2024 Bachelor Passes: 75%
  • Complete SASL immersion curriculum
  • Technical skills: Welding, woodworking
  • 100% NSC pass rate (2011-2024)
  • University preparation pathway
  • Deaf culture integration
Explore Deaf Program

Blind Learners Program

Braille Education & Independence Training

Education Span: Preprimary - FET (9 years)
Literacy Method: Braille & Adaptive Technology
Independence Focus: Mobility & Life Skills
  • Comprehensive Braille literacy
  • Orientation & mobility training
  • Adaptive technology integration
  • Daily living skills mastery
  • Traditional crafts training
Explore Blind Program

Autistic Learners Program

Structured Learning & Sensory Support

Program Structure: Level 1 - Skills Level 3
Class Ratio: 1:6 Teacher-Student
Approach: Individualized Education Plans
  • Structured, predictable environment
  • Visual learning supports
  • Sensory integration therapy
  • Communication development
  • Positive behavior support
